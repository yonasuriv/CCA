# Current thesis

**Electric aviation adoption is gated by operations and certification, not
by airframe supply or energy costs.**

Supporting evidence:

- Energy economics already favor electric on trial routes (~40% cost
  reduction at [Skyline Regional](../entities/skyline-regional.md)) --
  [source](../sources/2026-08-04-regional-electric-flight-trials.md)
- Utilization is capped by 45–60 min turnaround charging -- same source
- Analysts name certification the pacing item -- same source

Watch items that would challenge this thesis:

- A clean (methodologically sound) stall in
  [battery density progress](../concepts/battery-energy-density.md) would
  push technology back to the foreground. The current trend figure is
  disputed, so evidence is weak in both directions.
