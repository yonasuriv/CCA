# Overview — electric aviation (sample domain)

Current synthesis across all ingested sources.

Electric aviation for regional routes is moving from demonstration toward
early commercial trials. [Skyline Regional](entities/skyline-regional.md) is
the most advanced operator in our coverage, with five trial routes and
reported energy costs ~40% below turboprop baseline on its oldest routes
([source](sources/2026-08-04-regional-electric-flight-trials.md)).

The two pacing constraints are operational and regulatory, not airframe
supply: turnaround charging of 45–60 minutes caps daily utilization, and
analysts expect [certification timelines](concepts/certification-timelines.md)
to gate broader adoption. [Battery energy density](concepts/battery-energy-density.md)
is improving but the headline trend figure is
[disputed](concepts/battery-energy-density.md#contradictions--open-questions).
