# Skyline Regional

Regional carrier running the most advanced electric flight trial program in
our coverage.

## Key facts

- Five short-haul electric trial routes (under 250 km) as of 2026-08-03 —
  [source](../sources/2026-08-04-regional-electric-flight-trials.md)
- ~40% lower per-flight energy costs vs. turboprop baseline on its two
  oldest routes — [same source](../sources/2026-08-04-regional-electric-flight-trials.md)
- Turnaround charging (45–60 min) caps utilization at ~4 rotations/day —
  [same source](../sources/2026-08-04-regional-electric-flight-trials.md)
