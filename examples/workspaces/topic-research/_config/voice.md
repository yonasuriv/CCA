# Voice -- weekly brief

Role: factory. Configured once during setup; every brief follows it.

## Register

Direct, plain-spoken, no hype. Write for a busy domain lead who reads the
brief in under five minutes and decides what deserves attention.

## Structure expectations

- Lead with what changed this week, not background.
- One short paragraph per finding; a finding is a claim plus its evidence.
- Attribute everything: "per [source]" beats "studies show".

## Hard constraints

- Under 400 words total.
- No predictions without an explicit confidence note.
- No bullet point may contain more than one claim.
