# Regional carriers expand electric flight trials

*Sample source for the CCA example workspace -- synthetic content.*

Regional carrier Skyline Regional announced on 2026-08-03 that it will add
two more electric aircraft routes to its trial program, bringing the total
to five short-haul routes under 250 km. The carrier said early trial data
shows per-flight energy costs roughly 40% below its turboprop baseline on
the two oldest routes.

The article notes that turnaround charging remains the main operational
constraint: aircraft currently require 45–60 minutes of charging between
legs, limiting daily utilization to about four rotations per aircraft.

Industry analysts quoted in the piece expect certification timelines, not
airframe availability, to be the pacing item for broader adoption through
the decade.
