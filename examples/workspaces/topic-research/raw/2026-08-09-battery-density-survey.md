# Survey: battery energy density progress across aviation programs

*Sample source for the CCA example workspace — synthetic content.*

A survey of twelve electric aviation battery programs published this week
reports a median pack-level energy density of 285 Wh/kg among programs
currently in flight testing, up from a reported median of 260 Wh/kg in the
same survey last year.

The survey's authors caution that pack-level figures lag cell-level
announcements significantly, and that two programs declined to disclose
figures this year.

One quoted researcher disputes the year-over-year comparison, arguing that
changes in the survey's sample make the 260 → 285 Wh/kg trend "not a clean
measurement" of industry progress.
